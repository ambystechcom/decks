<Notes>
- Two assistants on two features in the same repo, side by side. That is the concrete use case.
- Each lane walks the phases on its own clock. The last segment cycles: implement, converge, implement, converge, until it reports converged.
- Review and converge are advisory by default. Nothing is modified unless you ask.
</Notes>
